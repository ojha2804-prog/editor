The macro exported 0 because of two bugs, not because Assem1 has no sheet metal.

1. VBScript used IsSheetMetal without (). That is a method. It always
   returned False, so Copy of SHEET METAL_* was skipped.
2. The VBA used GetPathName. SWOOD copies are virtual, path is empty,
   so those parts were skipped before export.

    → C:\Swood Reports\2026_09\Assem1\SheetMetalGeometry.vbs
