STAGE-08 — handle profiles 2500 / 2800 / 3000 mm on Bar Requirement

Tube stays 6000 mm. Handle / Gola / Pull materials nest on 2500 mm
by default. Open the page, then set Stock per material to 2500, 2800
or 3000.

Optional in swood-client.js CONFIG.weldments.stockByMaterial:
  'YOUR MATERIAL ID': 2800

Copy swood-client.js to DAT and the Assem1 report folder, Ctrl+F5.
Open Bar Requirement → Open → pick Stock on the handle row → Re-nest / Issue & lock.
