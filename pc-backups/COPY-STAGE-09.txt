STAGE-09 — native Qty back + no dinosaur on Bar Requirement

What went wrong
  To stop the main pages being disturbed I switched Product Quantity
  off for SWOOD's own pages (applyToAllPages: false). That is what made
  Sub Frames / Panels / Stocks / Saw / Sheetmetal all read Qty 1.
  Frames still read 2 because it has its own Product Qty column.

Fixed
  Product Quantity is applied to part and sub-assembly NB again, exactly
  as it was before the Glass & Mirror work. Verified: part NB 1 -> 2,
  NB 3 -> 6, sub-frame NB 1 -> 2, and running twice does not double it.
  Frames' own quantity is NOT touched, so the Frames page stays as it is.

Bar Requirement dinosaur
  #/weldment-bars had no SWOOD page behind it, so the not-found screen
  flashed until the overlay painted. A stub page is registered for that
  route and the overlay paints an opaque panel immediately.
